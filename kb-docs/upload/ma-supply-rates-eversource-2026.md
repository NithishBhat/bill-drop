# Eversource Eastern Massachusetts (NSTAR) residential Basic Service supply rates (as of October 2026)
Source: eversource-ma-rates-summary.pdf (Eversource MA rate summary, effective Sept 1, 2026)
Supply is the only part of the bill a customer can change, by switching from Basic Service to a municipal aggregation program (like BCCE in Boston) or a competitive supplier. Delivery is always paid to the utility.
- Eversource fixed residential Basic Service, Eastern MA (NEMA/SEMA):
  - January 2026: $0.14884/kWh
  - February 1 – July 31, 2026: $0.15629/kWh
  - August 1, 2026 – January 31, 2027: $0.17323/kWh  ← current rate
- Eversource Western MA (WCMA) fixed residential Basic Service: $0.13683/kWh (Feb–Jul 2026), $0.15934/kWh (from Aug 1, 2026)
- Residential Basic Service rates change every February 1 and August 1.
- Eversource R-1 residential customer charge: $10.00/month. Eversource also has R-1HP, a residential heat pump rate.
