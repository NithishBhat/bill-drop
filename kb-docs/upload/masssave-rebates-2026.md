# Mass Save residential rebates and offers (2026)

Mass Save is the Massachusetts energy-efficiency program run by the state's utilities (Eversource, National Grid, Unitil, Liberty, Berkshire Gas, Cape Light Compact). Offers below are for 2026; equipment must generally be installed between January 1 and December 31, 2026, with rebate paperwork due by February 28, 2027.
Note: summarized from masssave.com pages (the site blocks automated downloads). Verify amounts on masssave.com before giving financial advice.

## Home Energy Assessment (start here)
- No-cost home energy assessment; you get a customized report of the rebates you qualify for.
- Schedule: 866-527-SAVE (7283) or MassSave.com/HEA.
- Required first step for the enhanced (income-based) heat pump rebates.
- Source: https://www.masssave.com/residential/rebates-offers-services

## Insulation and air sealing
- 75%–100% off approved insulation improvements.
- No-cost targeted air sealing.
- Income-eligible owners and renters of 1–4 unit buildings may get these at no cost.
- Good fit when: winter energy use is much higher than summer use.

## Air-source heat pumps
- Whole-home: $2,650 per ton, up to $8,500.
- Partial-home: $1,125 per ton, up to $8,500.
- Basic: $250 per ton, up to $2,500.
- Total cap: $8,500 per account.
- Whole-home rebate requires the home to be sufficiently weatherized (home built in 2000 or later, OR the assessment recommends under $1,000 of work, OR weatherization recommended since 2013 has been completed).
- Partial-home customers who complete recommended weatherization get a $500 weatherization bonus.
- Must be installed by a Mass Save Heat Pump Installer Network contractor. From October 1, 2026, applications need a photo of each outdoor unit's serial-number nameplate.
- Good fit when: home heats with oil, propane, or electric resistance, or has high winter usage.
- Source: https://www.masssave.com/residential/rebates-offers-services/heating-and-cooling/heat-pumps/air-source-heat-pumps

## Air-to-water and ground-source heat pumps (income-based enhanced)
- Income-qualified households: up to $16,000 for air-to-water heat pumps; up to $25,000 for ground-source heat pumps.
- All weatherization recommended in the Home Energy Assessment must be completed first.
- Sources: https://www.masssave.com/residential/rebates-offers-services/heating-and-cooling/heat-pumps/air-to-water-heat-pumps , https://www.masssave.com/residential/rebates-offers-services/income-based-offers/save-with-enhanced-incentives/enhanced-heating-and-cooling-rebates

## Heat pump water heaters
- $750 instant rebate per ENERGY STAR unit (UEF 3.30); $1,500 for split systems.
- Installed by a licensed plumber in 2026; rebate cannot exceed purchase price.
- Get it via a participating distributor, at Home Depot or Lowe's with a code, or the Mass Save online marketplace.
- Good fit when: home has an electric-resistance water heater.
- Source: https://www.masssave.com/residential/rebates-offers-services/water-heating/heat-pump-water-heaters

## Smart and programmable thermostats
- Up to $100 per ENERGY STAR smart thermostat; limit 3 per account every 3 years.
- Up to $25 for a 7-day programmable thermostat.
- If it controls central AC or a heat pump, may also qualify for ConnectedSolutions incentives.
- Source: https://www.masssave.com/residential/rebates-offers-services/heating-and-cooling/smart-and-programmable-thermostats

## Financing
- HEAT Loan: financing up to $25,000 for qualified efficiency upgrades, terms up to 7 years.

## Income-eligible programs
- Income-qualified households can get many efficiency upgrades at no cost and higher (enhanced) rebates. Income limits are by household size on masssave.com.
- Source: https://www.masssave.com/residential/rebates-offers-services/income-based-offers
