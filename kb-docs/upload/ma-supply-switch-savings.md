# How to estimate yearly savings from switching electricity supply in Massachusetts
The electricity bill has two parts: delivery (always paid to the utility) and supply (the price per kWh of the electricity itself). Customers can only change the supply part.
yearly_savings = (current_supply_rate − new_supply_rate) × monthly_kWh × 12

Example: a Boston Eversource customer using 600 kWh/month on Basic Service ($0.17323) who switches to BCCE Basic ($0.13644) saves (0.17323 − 0.13644) × 600 × 12 ≈ $265/year.
