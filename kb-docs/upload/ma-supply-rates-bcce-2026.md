# Boston Community Choice Electricity (BCCE) supply rates, City of Boston (as of October 2026)
Sources: boston-bcce.html, boston-bcce-march-2026-letter.pdf
- Only for Eversource customers located in the City of Boston. Supplier: Direct Energy.
- BCCE rates locked from December 2025 to December 2027:
  - Optional Basic: $0.13644/kWh (cheapest)
  - Standard (default plan): $0.14214/kWh
  - Optional Green 100 (100% renewable): $0.16231/kWh
- New Eversource Basic Service accounts in Boston are auto-enrolled in BCCE Standard; customers can opt out or switch plans at no charge.
- Enroll / switch plans: boston.gov/bcce. Opt out: Direct Energy, 855-402-5868.
- From Feb 2021 to Dec 2025, BCCE households saved an average of $150/year vs. Eversource Basic Service. Savings are not guaranteed every billing cycle because Eversource rates change.
