# Massachusetts residential electricity supply rates (as of October 2026)

The electricity bill has two parts: **delivery** (always paid to the utility) and **supply** (the price per kWh of the electricity itself). Customers can only change the **supply** part, by switching from utility Basic Service to a municipal aggregation program (like BCCE in Boston) or a competitive supplier.

## Eversource Eastern Massachusetts (NSTAR) — Basic Service, Residential
Source: eversource-ma-rates-summary.pdf (Eversource MA rate summary, effective Sept 1, 2026)
- Fixed residential Basic Service, Eastern MA (NEMA/SEMA):
  - January 2026: $0.14884/kWh
  - February 1 – July 31, 2026: $0.15629/kWh
  - August 1, 2026 – January 31, 2027: $0.17323/kWh  ← current rate
- Western MA (WCMA) fixed residential Basic Service: $0.13683/kWh (Feb–Jul 2026), $0.15934/kWh (from Aug 1, 2026)
- Residential Basic Service rates change every February 1 and August 1.
- Eversource R-1 residential customer charge: $10.00/month.
- Eversource also has R-1HP, a residential heat pump rate.

## National Grid Massachusetts Electric — Basic Service, Residential
Source: nationalgrid-ma-basic-service-aug2026.pdf (M.D.P.U. No. 1-26-I)
- Residential (R-1, R-2) fixed-price Basic Service, August 2026 – January 2027: $0.17185/kWh total billed  ← current rate
- Residential monthly variable-price option (total billed per kWh): August $0.16074, September $0.15616, October $0.15615, November $0.16204, December $0.18477, January $0.20086.
- Customer service: 1-800-322-3223.

## Boston Community Choice Electricity (BCCE) — City of Boston
Sources: boston-bcce.html, boston-bcce-march-2026-letter.pdf
- Only for Eversource customers located in the City of Boston. Supplier: Direct Energy.
- Rates locked from December 2025 to December 2027:
  - Optional Basic: $0.13644/kWh (cheapest)
  - Standard (default plan): $0.14214/kWh
  - Optional Green 100 (100% renewable): $0.16231/kWh
- New Eversource Basic Service accounts in Boston are auto-enrolled in BCCE Standard; customers can opt out or switch plans at no charge.
- Enroll / switch plans: boston.gov/bcce. Opt out: Direct Energy, 855-402-5868.
- From Feb 2021 to Dec 2025, BCCE households saved an average of $150/year vs. Eversource Basic Service. Savings are not guaranteed every billing cycle because Eversource rates change.

## How to estimate yearly savings from switching supply
yearly_savings = (current_supply_rate − new_supply_rate) × monthly_kWh × 12

Example: a Boston Eversource customer using 600 kWh/month on Basic Service ($0.17323) who switches to BCCE Basic ($0.13644) saves (0.17323 − 0.13644) × 600 × 12 ≈ $265/year.
